Regular cues: converted to mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV,
low-pass filtered at 3.5 kHz, with a 3 ms fade-in and 15 ms fade-out.
Playback gain is reduced further by the game's sound controller.
These assets are bundled locally; no external audio service is used.

Celebrations are original arrangements of the same CC0 pluck_001.ogg,
using ascending major arpeggios and a final four-note chord. No additional
recordings or licensed music were used. Reproduction details (FFmpeg):

1. Decode pluck_001.ogg and apply aformat=channel_layouts=mono. Split one
   copy per note. Each note is (start milliseconds, semitone shift, gain).

   win.wav (1.75 seconds):
   (0,-12,.90), (160,-8,.90), (320,-5,.90), (510,0,.88), (700,4,.86),
   (900,7,.84), (1120,0,.44), (1120,4,.44), (1120,7,.44), (1120,12,.30)

   match-win.wav (2.20 seconds):
   (0,-12,.90), (120,-8,.90), (240,-5,.90), (400,0,.88), (560,4,.86),
   (720,7,.84), (900,12,.78), (1020,7,.78), (1140,4,.78), (1300,12,.84),
   (1510,0,.45), (1510,4,.45), (1510,7,.45), (1510,12,.34)

2. For each note, set RATE = round(44100 * 2^(semitone shift / 12)),
   GAIN = note gain, DELAY = start milliseconds, and apply this filter:
   asetrate=RATE,aresample=44100,lowpass=f=3500,afade=t=in:d=0.003,
   areverse,afade=t=in:d=0.015,areverse,
   aecho=0.8:0.6:95|190|285:0.24|0.12|0.06,volume=GAIN,adelay=DELAY:all=1

3. Mix with amix=inputs=NOTE_COUNT:normalize=0,apad,atrim=duration=DURATION,
   afade=t=out:st=FADE_START:d=0.18, where FADE_START = DURATION - 0.18.
   Render mono float32 at 44100 Hz. Normalize to absolute peak 0.72
   (both current renders use volume=1.624332308769), then encode as
   16-bit PCM WAV. This leaves headroom and a soft tail without clipping.

4. Game playback volume: win 0.34, match-win 0.36. Regular cue gains are
   unchanged (0.22-0.28). New ordinary cues cannot cut a celebration short;
   mute, updates while hidden, disconnect, reset and teardown stop playback.

Draw-card impacts are also original CC0 arrangements, with a reversed,
filtered scratch building into a pitched-down bong. The bass impact peaks
at 0.506 seconds for +4 and 0.365 seconds for +2, to follow the card flight.
Both have mono 44100 Hz, 16-bit PCM output and absolute peak 0.72.

Apply to bong_001.ogg:
aformat=channel_layouts=mono,asetrate=BODY_RATE,aresample=44100,
highpass=f=45,lowpass=f=900,afade=t=in:d=0.006,
areverse,afade=t=in:d=0.040,areverse,
aecho=0.8:0.7:ECHO_DELAYS:ECHO_DECAYS,volume=BODY_GAIN,
adelay=BODY_DELAY_MS:all=1,asetpts=N/SR/TB

Apply to the air source:
aformat=channel_layouts=mono,asetrate=AIR_RATE,aresample=44100,
highpass=f=160,lowpass=f=AIR_LOWPASS,areverse,asetpts=N/SR/TB,
afade=t=in:d=AIR_RISE_SECONDS,
afade=t=out:st=AIR_FALL_START:d=AIR_FALL_SECONDS,volume=AIR_GAIN

Mix the two tracks with amix=inputs=2:normalize=0, then
apad=whole_len=COUNT,atrim=end_sample=COUNT,asetpts=N/SR/TB,
afade=t=out:st=FADE_START:d=0.13
where COUNT = round(duration * 44100) and FADE_START = duration - 0.13.
Render float32, apply the final normalization above, and encode PCM WAV.

Playback uses 0.34 volume for +4 and 0.30 for +2. Each additional card
owed in the stack adds 0.004 volume (maximum extra 0.04) and reduces
playbackRate by 0.01 (minimum 0.86, preservesPitch=false). Weight resets
for a new unstacked card. Impacts outrank turn cues, fresh stacked cards
can replace the preceding impact, and celebrations outrank every impact.
